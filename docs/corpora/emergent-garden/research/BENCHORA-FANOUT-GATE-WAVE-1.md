# Benchora Fanout Gate — Wave 1

**Campaign:** `eg-nested-corpus-2026-09`  
**Destination:** `KooshaPari/Benchora`  
**Decision:** permit one documentation-only draft experiment contract

## Why this fanout is permitted

Benchora already owns benchmark execution, pinned baselines, regression comparison, and mutation-oriented testing. Wave 1 contains direct, revision-pinned evidence that the relevant creator projects rely on repeated execution, external scoring, incumbent retention, failure artifacts, and comparison across coordination/search strategies.

The proposed downstream change is therefore within Benchora's existing job. It does not ask Benchora to become a corpus store, agent runtime, game runner, or orchestration framework.

## Evidence used

- Wave 1 source manifest and generated digest;
- Mindcraft evidence chain and primary paper pointer;
- `fractalsearch` recursive research loop;
- `AgentsOfEmpires` tournament/status/artifact loop;
- `agent_prompts` shared-artifact coordination experiment;
- current Benchora benchmark/baseline/regression contract.

## Allowed destination change

A draft research/experiment specification covering coordination topology under constant task conditions, with controls, negative controls, metrics, abort conditions, evidence bundle, and explicit non-goals.

## Forbidden destination change

- no benchmark implementation in this wave;
- no model/provider integration;
- no Minecraft/Age of Empires dependency;
- no Agentora or thegent authority claim;
- no product-code change;
- no release/version claim;
- no representation of creator results as reproduced.

## Staleness

The downstream draft becomes stale when the source-manifest digest, claim set, Benchora default-branch contract, or experiment owner changes.
