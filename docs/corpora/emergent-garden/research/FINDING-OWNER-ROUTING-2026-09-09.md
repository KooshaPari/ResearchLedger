# Finding-to-owner routing inventory -- 2026-09-09

## Scope and independent counts

This is a docs-only routing inventory, not transfer authorization or a product
plan. It uses the existing corpus and its original 18-work-package audit only;
it does not acquire sources, inspect destination repositories, or infer an
owner from a project reference.

The 18 WPs in
[`PROGRAM-AUDIT-AND-WBS-2026-09-08.md`](PROGRAM-AUDIT-AND-WBS-2026-09-08.md#original-18-work-package-program)
and the nine named applicability surfaces below are independent counts. The
nine surfaces are not a numerator, subset, or completion fraction of the 18
WPs. The WPs remain corpus, acquisition, reproduction, publication, or
delivery gates under the canonical ResearchLedger campaign.

`VERIFIED` means the cited section of
`phenotype-registry/BOUNDARY_OWNERS.md` names the owner. `HISTORICAL` means
the corpus assigns a role but the inspected registry boundary does not.
`UNKNOWN` is unresolved and must not be routed. `MISSING` means not linked in
the inspected corpus; destination existence is unverified because no
destination repository was inspected.

ResearchLedger remains canonical for research, provenance, claims, and
projections. CI, scanner, publication, and merge matters are delivery detours,
not research findings, and are excluded.
